# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. We want this project to be a place where people of any background can ask questions and send fixes.

- Be respectful. No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Assume good intent, and say clearly what you mean. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give feedback that helps the other person improve their work.
- Do not publish other people's private information, including logins, tokens and personal data found in logs.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep
doing it. To report a problem, contact the maintainers at the address in SECURITY.md (use the subject "Conduct").
This code is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
