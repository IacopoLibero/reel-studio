# Reel questions: strong psychological hooks

How to use: `[x]` = reel already made (with its folder), `[ ]` = to do. When a reel is finished, tick its box.
The reel opens with the question. After the question, the hook word says why it works.
Source = where the facts come from: a blog post slug, a file path, a URL. "needs source" = nothing covers it yet:
give a document or text before making it, or the reel stays with well-known facts and no numbers.

Hooks used: **fear** (what you could lose), **curiosity** (a gap to close), **myth** (a belief to break), **money**, **effort** (how easy it is),
**identity** (what kind of owner you are), **proof** (what others do), **number** (a clear list or count).
